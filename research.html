Research and projects

I design GNC systems for spacecraft and launchers, from guidance, navigation and control algorithms to FDIR, simulation, and verification and validation. A common thread is making optimization-based guidance and control fast and reliable enough to run onboard, in real time, with guarantees. Below are the main projects I have contributed to at Indra Space (formerly DEIMOS Space).

Active debris removal: ClearSpace-1 (ESA, ongoing)

ClearSpace-1 is ESA's flagship mission to capture and remove a piece of space debris. I am the technical lead for the close-range GNC.

  • 6-DoF motion-synchronization guidance based on onboard optimization, enabling the capture of a tumbling target.

  • Autocodable interior-point solver for convex conic optimization.

  • Explicit optimal thruster allocation with guaranteed worst-case execution time and minimum-impulse-bit handling (IEEE TCST 2025).

  • Rendezvous guidance with relative orbital elements, attitude guidance, robust control synthesis and FDIR safety monitors.

Onboard optimal control for safe rendezvous (PhD, 2022–2026)

Industrial PhD at Instituto Superior Técnico, carried out at Indra Space and funded by a Portuguese Space Agency / FCT fellowship. The thesis develops model predictive control methods for non-cooperative rendezvous that remain safe under uncertainty and are cheap enough to run onboard:

  • Robust and stochastic tube-based MPC, and MPC based on System Level Synthesis.

  • Custom, structure-exploiting optimization solvers (including operator-splitting methods).

  • A high-fidelity rendezvous simulator, including an angles-only relative navigation filter (EKF), used to validate the guidance and control.

Results appeared in IEEE TCST, ACC, EuroGNC and the ESA GNC & ICATT conference, with more to be published; see Publications.

Reusable launchers

  • RETALT and SALTO (European Commission): GNC for the recovery of a reusable launch vehicle, matured to TRL 4 (processor-in-the-loop), with onboard guidance based on lossless and successive convexification.

  • SURE (ESA): as work package lead, significantly reduced the onboard computational cost of recovery guidance relative to the state of the art, using successive convexification with custom first-order methods (xPIPG).

  • AI4GNC (ESA): cut the compute load of onboard optimal guidance by an order of magnitude with a learned surrogate model.

  • VALORS (ESA, ongoing): supervising the work package on reachability analysis for worst-case verification and validation of launcher GNC.

  • REVLAN (ESA): real-time parafoil guidance based on onboard optimization.
